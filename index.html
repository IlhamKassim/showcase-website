About the Project

Reber Building Hallway Renovation
Reber Building · Penn State
Project Overview

A New Hallway Experience for the Department of Mechanical Engineering

The Kinetic Engineering Collective set out to redesign and revitalize the hallway of the Reber Building at Penn State's College of Engineering. The goal: create a welcoming, informative, and visually compelling space that represents the innovation and excellence of the Mechanical Engineering department.

Our capstone project pairs the physical renovation with a fully immersive 360° virtual tour — letting prospective students, faculty, and remote visitors experience the improved hallway from anywhere in the world.

Our Goal

Redesign the Reber Building hallway to better reflect the identity of Penn State's Mechanical Engineering department and provide an accessible virtual experience for all audiences.

What We Did

Redesigned signage, installed modern displays, improved lighting, and created an immersive web-based 360° virtual tour using Pannellum and equirectangular panoramic photography.

Who Benefits

Prospective students, current students, alumni, faculty, and remote stakeholders who want to experience the Mechanical Engineering facilities at Penn State without being physically present.

Design Process

From brainstorming to the final product
Step 01

Brainstorming & Ideation

The team gathered to identify pain points in the current Reber Building hallway — outdated signage, poor wayfinding, lack of visual identity. We generated ideas through mind-mapping, sketching, and referencing best-in-class engineering department showcases.

Step 02

Research & Planning

We researched materials, design systems, budgeting constraints, and departmental brand guidelines. A project plan with milestones and assigned responsibilities was created to keep the team on track throughout the semester.

Step 03

Design & Prototyping

Using digital design tools, we created mockups and prototypes of the hallway redesign elements — signage layouts, display positions, and material choices. Feedback from faculty and peers shaped the final design direction.

Step 04

Procurement

Within our $1,250 budget, we sourced all necessary materials and components. This included display materials, mounting hardware, signage printing, and any technology needed for the installation. Total spent: $1,150.

Step 05

Installation

The team physically implemented the hallway improvements in the Reber Building. This involved coordinating with building management, precise measurements, installation of all display and signage elements, and on-site problem-solving.

Step 06

360° Photography & Virtual Tour

After installation, we captured the hallway using equirectangular 360° photography. The images were processed and loaded into our Pannellum-based virtual tour, complete with interactive hotspots and multi-scene navigation.

Step 07

Final Showcase

The completed hallway and virtual tour were presented at Penn State's Capstone Showcase — demonstrating both the physical renovation and the web-based immersive experience to students, faculty, and guests.

Did You Know?

Fun facts about the project & its impact

Why This Project Matters

The Reber Building hallway renovation and virtual tour supports Penn State's Mechanical Engineering department by creating a more welcoming, informative, and accessible space — for students, faculty, and the broader community.

01

Virtual Tours Boost Engagement

Studies show that virtual tours increase engagement with physical facilities by more than 3× — making our digital walkthrough a powerful recruitment and outreach tool for the ME department.

02

Accessible Anytime, Anywhere

Prospective students from around the world can now explore the Reber Building hallway without travelling to State College — opening the department to a truly global audience.

03

Open-Source Technology

Our virtual tour is powered by Pannellum, an open-source WebGL panoramic viewer — the same technology used by thousands of institutions for interactive virtual experiences.

04

The Reber Building

The Reber Building is home to Penn State's Department of Mechanical Engineering — one of the top-ranked ME programs in the United States.

05

Student-Led, Real Impact

Every element of this project — from physical installation to the web experience — was designed, built, and delivered by undergraduate students as their capstone project.

06

Under Budget, On Time

The team completed the full hallway renovation and virtual tour within the allocated $1,250 budget, spending $1,150 — delivering a high-quality result with $100 to spare.
